A concise participant-only workflow for confirming the correct event rules, submitting readable original battle evidence, checking scores and raising disputes. It avoids administrator commands and implementation details.

## 1. Before the event

<div style="background-color:#DFF2FF; border:1.5px solid #0070C0; padding:6px 8px; margin:4px 0;"><strong>⚓ Quick hint:</strong> Preserve the original evidence and distinguish an observed fact from an OCR, bot, or calculator interpretation.</div>
1. Open the exact Pilot or Recurring ruleset named in the latest official notice. Check event time zone, arrival cutoff, map/mode, lineup rules, evidence route, settings version, and help contact.
2. Confirm that your in-game name matches the published roster or identity mapping. Ask the organizer to correct a mismatch before play.
3. Join the official communications route and keep the notice/support method available. Each team should have a primary and backup representative.

## 2. Before ready-up

<div style="background-color:#DFF2FF; border:1.5px solid #0070C0; padding:6px 8px; margin:4px 0;"><strong>⚓ Quick hint:</strong> Verify room configuration and both sides’ readiness before starting; record exceptions as they occur.</div>
1. Check the fixture ID, opponent, game number, map, mode, duration, team/ship limits and lineup visibility.
2. Wait until both team representatives confirm the room settings and legal lineup. Raise a mismatch before ready-up; do not assume the host’s check is enough.
3. Know the separate clocks for attendance, between-game wait, restart request and restart completion. Use only the event’s published limits.

## 3. Capture and submit evidence

<div style="background-color:#DFF2FF; border:1.5px solid #0070C0; padding:6px 8px; margin:4px 0;"><strong>⚓ Quick hint:</strong> Apply the declared clock and restart rule for this event; record the trigger, time, and decision.</div>
1. After the battle, capture the original readable Personal Score/result screen required by the event. Include the visible player identity and the fields the event requires.
2. Do not crop out relevant values, paint over text, or edit the numeric result to make it agree with the calculator. If the image is unclear, retain it and request a clear original.
3. Submit through the event’s officially designated Discord channel/form or approved route. If the bot is unavailable, follow the published manual fallback. Do not send screenshots to an unverified private account.
4. If OCR text or a bot reply appears, compare it against the original screenshot. OCR is not the official score until accepted by the event’s recordkeeper.

## 4. What you should verify

<div style="background-color:#DFF2FF; border:1.5px solid #0070C0; padding:6px 8px; margin:4px 0;"><strong>⚓ Quick hint:</strong> Preserve the original evidence and distinguish an observed fact from an OCR, bot, or calculator interpretation.</div>
| Field | What to check |
| --- | --- |
| Player / clan or alias | Matches your identity mapping and the correct game. |
| Battle Result | Victory/Defeat or draw as shown on the original result. |
| Map / mode | Matches the visible battle record and fixture. |
| Battle start time | Date/time is associated with the correct game. |
| Damage | Use the official post-battle Damage statistic specified by the rules; not potential damage. |
| Credits | Required DWBOT field; compare with the original result screen. |\n| Free XP | Required DWBOT field; compare with the original result screen. |\n| XP | Required DWBOT field; compare with the original result screen. |\n| Record completeness | DWBOT requires all nine fields: result, player, map, mode, battleStart, credits, freeXp, damage and xp. Missing or unreadable values remain unresolved; do not guess or fill them from another source. |

## 5. If something is wrong

<div style="background-color:#DFF2FF; border:1.5px solid #0070C0; padding:6px 8px; margin:4px 0;"><strong>⚓ Quick hint:</strong> Historical material provides context only; do not present it as current validation or approval.</div>
1. Do not silently overwrite a disputed value or ask anyone to alter the screenshot. Preserve the original evidence and the bot/calculator response.
2. Send the match/game ID, disputed field, original screenshot, extracted value, and a short explanation to the official correction route.
3. If accepted metrics, a scoring output, the calculated match outcome, or the official record conflict, pause outcome publication and bracket advancement until the inputs/formula are reconciled and the referee or authorized decision-maker rules. Pause any affected individual score or award publication too.
4. Follow the published appeal deadline and include the opponent’s response where requested. A correction must retain the old value, new value, reviewer, reason and timestamp.

## 6. Definition of done

<div style="background-color:#DFF2FF; border:1.5px solid #0070C0; padding:6px 8px; margin:4px 0;"><strong>⚓ Quick hint:</strong> Preserve the original evidence and distinguish an observed fact from an OCR, bot, or calculator interpretation.</div>
> **Submission complete when**: The original readable screenshot is linked to the correct participant and game, required values have been checked, the official recordkeeper has confirmed acceptance or requested a specific correction, and any dispute is recorded. A bot response alone is not proof that the record is accepted.

## Important policy notes

- Recurring bracket advancement uses the verified match outcome calculated from published individual-performance metrics under the approved scoring method, with D21 tie-breaks applied where needed; do not use a raw participant `dblns` value alone unless the published formula expressly specifies that use.
- Pilot Damage ranking determines only the individual awards expressly announced for that Pilot.
- The default participant-facing release suppresses H12; the historical $12.56 diagnostic is not cash value, official store price, prize valuation, or an award obligation.


## 7. Know which tournament stage you are entering

The latest official event notice controls. A reusable ruleset or planning proposal is not an event notice.

| Stage | What participants should expect | What does not carry over automatically |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Pilot | Attendance/drop-in baseline, a published arrival cutoff, organizer-formed lineup, event-specific map/mode, evidence route and any announced individual awards | Pilot attendance or Damage placement does not automatically qualify or seed anyone for a season or playoffs |
| Regular season | Individual registration, published eligibility, roster/team assignment, weekly fixture, map/mode/ship allowlist, match schedule, evidence and standings process | Do not assume a previous week's roster, map, lineup, or exception applies to the next fixture |
| Playoffs | Qualification and seeding only from the verified standings and the published qualification/tie-break rules; follow the announced bracket, series length, host and substitution rules | Do not assume a top-four/top-eight bracket, best-of series, bye, or tie-break not stated in the notice |
| Grand YOLO season finale | A separately announced season-ending championship event with its own entrants, format, schedule, scoring/outcome rules, awards and evidence process | Do not assume it is the same thing as the calculator's Grand Yolo Round 4, or that regular-season standings alone determine the champion |

Before committing to an event, confirm the exact event stage, time zone, registration/arrival cutoff, eligibility, team/roster rules, map/mode and ship limits, prize details, official evidence route, referee/help contact, dispute deadline, and settings/rules version. If any required item is blank or contradictory, ask the organizer before play.
