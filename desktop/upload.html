What happens next?

The screenshot is checked against the nine mandatory fields: result, player, map, mode, battle start, credits, Free XP, damage and XP. No fuzzy value repair is applied. Missing or conflicting values require an exact correction before mapping.

Desktop uploads use the same Tesseract-first pipeline as Discord attachments. OCR.space remains disabled unless separately configured and authorized; no image is sent to an external OCR provider by this page.
