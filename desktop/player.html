Pilot YOLO and Full YOLO

The same participant and evidence rules apply; the event lifecycle is different.

One single-evening event. Draft, publish, play and complete. Pilot results do not automatically seed a Full YOLO season.

A multi-week or multi-month season with Regular Season, Postseason and Grand YOLO Finale. Dates, cadence and advancement rules are published by organizers.
