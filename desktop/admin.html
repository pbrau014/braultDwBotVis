TOURNAMENT FORMAT

Pilot YOLO or Full YOLO

Both formats share participant workflows. Pilot is one evening; Full is a declared season with regular season, postseason and Grand YOLO finale.

Quick help - format differences

Pilot: no season or postseason. Full: declare season duration; the three stages are created automatically. Legacy Damage, Kills, Race and Grand Yolo remain score categories, not stages. Unapproved scoring and event values must remain blank.
