 					
						
🎮 **WELCOME TO THE YOLO TOURNEY!** 🏆

Fight bots and humans for prizes while accumulating damage and W’s over several weeks!

💥 Damage enemies to progress through the tournament bracket. **Rankings and prizes are based on individual damage, not team W’s.**

👑 At the end of the season, the **Grand YOLO Tourney** will be held! Final placement and prizes are based on your accumulated damage from previous weeks and the finale.

🤝 **NEW TEAMS EVERY WEEK!**
Teams are created through a random draft. You’ll play with your assigned teammates for one evening, with a new draft each week.

⚔️ **EACH TOURNAMENT NIGHT**
• 3 rounds, with approximately 7 games per player.
• Round 1: Fight bots.
• Rounds 2–3: Fight teams of humans and bots.
• Every team includes human and bot players.

⏱️ **TIME COMMITMENT**
The entire evening should take no more than 3 hours, with breaks between rounds.

💰 **DEAL DAMAGE. EARN REWARDS. CLIMB THE RANKINGS.**

Good luck, and may you only live once! 🚀
