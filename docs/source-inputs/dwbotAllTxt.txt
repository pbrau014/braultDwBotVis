GOVERNING COMPUTER-VISION-ASSISTED OCR PRINCIPLE
======================================================================

A computer-vision-assisted OCR pipeline converts images, PDFs, and photos into structured, machine-readable data through preprocessing, text localization, recognition, layout analysis, parsing, and validation. DWBOT applies this model primarily to World of Warships screenshots, where screen type, resolution, UI scale, and game layout—not generic document assumptions—govern extraction.

| Stage | Governing function |
|---|---|
| 1. Preprocess (CV) | Preserve the original; correct orientation/deskew only when justified (e.g., Hough transforms or projection profiles); denoise and use measured thresholding (e.g., adaptive/Otsu); locate/crop regions of interest (ROIs). |
| 2. Detect/localize | Find text, anchors, rows, columns, and regions using suitable deep-learning detectors (e.g., CRAFT/EAST/YOLO) or classical contours/connected components. |
| 3. Recognize | Read localized words/lines with Tesseract's LSTM-based baseline; evaluate LSTM/CTC, CNN, transformer, or other recognizers independently and verify engine/version behavior. Preserve raw text, geometry, engine/model, and confidence. |
| 4. Analyze/layout | Restore reading order and row/column/cell relationships; parse fields; cross-check values; validate exact rules; flag uncertainty for human review. |

Use modular detection, recognition, and parsing for control, speed, and measurable field-level validation. End-to-end vision-language models (VLMs) may produce structured output directly, but can cost more, run slower, and reduce stage-level control; benchmark them as alternatives, never bypassing provenance, exact validation, or review. For DWBOT, stable UI anchors, screenshot profiles, row/cell geometry, field-specific crops, and exact-value acceptance take priority. Generic multi-column document, PDF, and form processing remains a separate optional profile.

The supplied source includes citation markers [1]–[3] but no bibliography; treat them as unresolved references until their sources are identified.

4. DWBOT MISSION, SCOPE, AND BASELINE
======================================================================

4.1 Mission

Build and maintain an accurate, reliable, secure, maintainable tournament system that receives World of Warships battle screenshots through Discord and/or Player HTML, extracts validated battle data, preserves tournament scoring and persistence, and provides separate Player and Admin interfaces. The bot remains authoritative for tournament data, calculations, permissions, and score validation.

4.2 Preserve existing behavior

Preserve current Discord commands/events, submission flow, player/event mapping, scoring, workbook formulas, SQLite/JSON persistence, recovery, output formatting, and established interfaces unless an evidence-backed, authorized change is necessary. The system is not a generic invoice OCR application.

4.3 Core design

- Existing Node.js Discord bot remains the application baseline.
- Tesseract.js remains the primary baseline engine.
- OpenCV performs measurable CV/localization/preprocessing work.
- PaddleOCR remains an independently tested candidate; the tested hybrid reportedly regressed, so retain its fallback disabled (`PADDLEOCR_FIELD_FALLBACK=0`) until new same-fixture evidence justifies enabling it.
- Free network/API services may be used as primary or secondary recognition paths only when necessary/appropriate, genuinely free, acceptable under privacy/security/terms, and demonstrably useful. Local/offline processing must remain viable.
- Preserve raw image, crop, transformation, candidate, validation, and review evidence.
- Human review is the safe outcome when evidence is insufficient.

4.4 Supported OCR profiles

1. **Primary: WoWS screenshots.** Predictable game UI profiles, scoreboard rows/columns, game statistics and metadata.
2. **Optional: generic document OCR.** PDFs, TIFFs, photos, multi-page documents, multi-column layouts, generic tables/forms and document exports. Keep its schema, processing assumptions, dependencies, and acceptance separate; never impose invoice/document assumptions on WoWS records.

2. `player`
3. `map`
4. `mode`
5. `battleStart`
6. `credits`

6.2 Expand to all reliably identifiable text fields

Add any further text/metadata field whose on-screen label, title, heading, identifier, and associated value can be interpreted and extracted reliably and meaningfully. Do not weaken the nine mandatory fields.

Every field requires: stable ID; label; type; required/optional/conditional status; screen/layout/ROI mapping; OCR/preprocessing profile; parsing/validation; source/ground-truth tests; provenance; schema version; output compatibility.

Classify fields as:
- Core required: the nine fields above.
- Supported optional: reliable extra text/metadata.
- Conditional: shown only on specific screens/modes/layouts.
- Unsupported/ambiguous: retain evidence, mark unavailable/uncertain, and do not invent a value.

Enable a new field only after label/value mapping, localization, meaning, schema, validation, representative data, positive/negative/ambiguous/missing tests, backward compatibility, and documentation all pass. Keep raw text, parsed candidate, selected/accepted value, and human correction separate.

6.3 End-to-end WoWS OCR pipeline

1. **Ingest:** accept supported Discord/HTML uploads; enforce 25 MB; validate actual bytes, signature, format and decodability; stream and incrementally SHA-256 hash; stage safely; capture filename, media type, dimensions, source and Discord message/attachment IDs.
2. **Classify:** identify screenshot type, layout, resolution, UI scale, and supported game screen. Reject or review unknown/corrupt input.
3. **Localize:** use stable anchors, templates, coordinates and OpenCV geometry to find scoreboard, teams, rows, player/ship/stat columns, cells and metadata. Preserve row/column identity, bounding boxes, original coordinates and source mapping.
4. **Preprocess:** preserve original; create reproducible field-specific crops/variants for names, ships, numbers, icons and metadata. Evaluate scaling, contrast, denoise, sharpen, grayscale, threshold, and morphology per use. Avoid universal Otsu, whole-image rotation, deskew/unwarp, or fixed 2480 px scaling unless data justifies it.
5. **Recognize:** call independent Tesseract.js and PaddleOCR adapters; optionally call an approved free OCR/API service when needed. Preserve raw text, boxes/polygons, confidence, engine/model/version/configuration, errors, crop, and transformation metadata.
6. **Analyze layout:** assign OCR words to rows/columns/cells using coordinates, boundaries, centers, overlap/IoU, anchors and field type. For bordered tables use morphological line detection where useful; for borderless tables use spatial projection and word-coordinate alignment. Flag overlapping/missing/out-of-bounds cells.
7. **Parse:** map text and geometry to the field registry, keeping raw text and parsed form separate.
8. **Validate:** apply exact field rules, format/range/reference/cross-field checks and independent evidence. Engine agreement supports but does not prove correctness. Fuzzy names/ship lists may suggest for review only.
9. **Resolve:** accept only supported values; otherwise mark ambiguous/missing/rejected and route for review. No guessed values or silent autocorrection.
10. **Persist/output:** retain image, hashes, crops, variants, candidates, scores, decisions, corrections, audit history and reviewer data. Export only approved records to existing DWBOT/Discord workflows.
11. **Observe/recover:** record per-image progress, errors, timing/resources, queue/job state; resume safely without loss or duplicate side effects.

6.4 Recognition and selection rules

- Compare candidates field-by-field; calibrate confidence independently per engine and field.
- Do not compare unlike engine confidence scores as if they were equivalent.
- Preserve all candidates, including low-confidence and rejected ones.
- Do not choose the largest, most common, highest-confidence, or most plausible number without field-specific proof.
- Never autocorrect player/ship names or ambiguous digits. Roster/dictionary matches are review evidence only.
- Keep exact punctuation/case unless a documented normalization rule permits it.
- Preserve `raw_text`, `parsed_candidate`, `selected_value`, `accepted_value`, and `review_correction` as distinct.
- Record rationale, validation/rejection, reviewer, timestamp, and any correction.

**Phase 16 — Metrics, health and alerting**

**Phase 17 — Packaging and containers**
Reproducible local install/start; Docker/Compose where justified; non-root, multi-stage, native dependencies, models, permissions, mounts, health checks and resource limits. Test clean extraction/install and actual runtime.
