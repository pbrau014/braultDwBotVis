# v3.18 Change Log and Visual Style Specification

> <span style="color:#244c70">⚓ **Quick hint:** Check the stated authority, required evidence, and completion criteria before acting; do not infer an approval that is not recorded.</span>


## Design decisions applied
- Chapter title style: 25 pt Aptos Display, navy, bold, shadow; next-level numbered subsection target: 12.5 pt, giving a 2:1 relationship. Document title remains 28 pt.
- Appendix title style: 24 pt Aptos Display, navy, bold, shadow. Top-level contents/index entries: 24 pt for chapter references and 23 pt for appendix references. Contents heading: 27 pt.
- Appendix text is reduced by 2 pt from the original effective style where applicable. The title heading remains 24 pt. Table text is not reduced more than 3 pt below the pre-redesign smallest intentional text; wider columns and wrapping take precedence over unreadable one-line fitting.
- Table header fill: light sky blue (`#BDE7FF`); alternating body rows white / very pale blue; internal borders slightly darker; outside border thick and dark; heavier lower/right outside edges provide a Word-compatible shadow-like treatment.
- Numbered subsection headings receive a thick black top separator and controlled paragraph spacing.
- Chapter separators use a full line of repeated anchor icons and black border rules, per selected Decision A2. This is an explicit exception to the ordinary one-icon-per-line rule.
- “Done when” paragraphs use a light-blue background and solid deeper-blue border, with one green checkbox at the beginning. Text/icon size is 6.5 pt (the v3.17 9.5 pt baseline reduced by the requested 3 pt); this is small for print and is disclosed in QA.
- Icon hygiene: ordinary paragraphs retain at most one icon; numbered procedure entries use a consistent heading-related icon within each list where source lines already had icons; heading icons are 3 pt larger than heading text and numbered-list icons are 1 pt smaller than surrounding text.
- Paragraph spacing is style-based rather than padded with empty paragraphs; main-body paragraph spacing is applied outside tables, callouts, lists, and appendix records.
- Markdown preserves semantic heading/table/callout structure and normalized icon choices; it does not pretend to reproduce Word shadows, exact font sizes, or rounded corners.

## Format limitations
Editable Word tables do not provide reliable native rounded corners or HTML-like drop shadows. Word/PDF table treatment uses the thick dark outer border and heavier lower/right edge as the shadow-like treatment. The exact score reporter HTML is missing, so its CSS rounded-corner/shadow treatment could not be updated.
