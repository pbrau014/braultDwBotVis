## Scope and method
All pages of the three newly generated v3.17 PDFs were freshly rendered to page images. Numbered contact sheets were assembled and visually inspected page by page, including each document's first and final pages. This review applies to the new PDFs, not merely the v3.15 source PDFs.

- `01_Participant/Grand_Tournament_Participant_Guide_v3.17.pdf` — 14 pages.
- `02_Operations/Grand_Tournament_Event_Operations_Manual_v3.17.pdf` — 23 pages.
- `03_Technical_Reference/Grand_Tournament_Technical_Reference_v3.17.pdf` — 30 pages.

**Total inspected:** 67 pages across 3 PDFs. **Contact sheets:** 9.

## Findings
- No obvious text clipping, overlapping blocks, missing pages, or broken page renders were observed in the contact-sheet review.
- Heading hierarchy and section breaks are consistent, and the new role-based contents links are visible.
- The technical reference and several appendices are intentionally dense. For print use, readers should inspect tables and small text at normal/full page size; contact-sheet inspection is a layout screen, not a guarantee of readability at every printer setting.
- The visual pass does not certify substantive correctness, scoring implementation, legal/accessibility compliance, or event approval.
