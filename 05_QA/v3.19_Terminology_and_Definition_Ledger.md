# v3.19 Terminology and Definition Ledger

<div style="background-color:#DFF2FF; border:1.5px solid #0070C0; padding:6px 8px; margin:4px 0;"><strong>⚓ Quick hint:</strong> Use the defined term consistently; if two documents differ, follow the explicitly named governing source.</div>
This ledger governs wording across the current role-based publications and companion materials. Historical records are explicitly identified as such; this ledger does not retroactively rewrite v3.15/v3.17 baselines.

| Preferred term | Definition / usage rule | Avoid confusing it with | Status/evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Verified match outcome** | The accepted outcome for a specific match, calculated from individual performance across the applicable published game metrics under the approved scoring method; published forfeit/void rules apply where performance scoring does not. It governs recurring bracket advancement with the published tie-break rules. | A single raw metric, an overview average, or an unverified calculator result. | Governing rule for this v3.21 editorial candidate; exact implementation remains unverified. |
| **Official result** | The validated official record entered by the authorized recordkeeper, based on required evidence. | OCR extraction before human validation. | Existing glossary definition retained and cross-checked. |
| **Individual `dblns` output** | A participant-level calculated performance output; its contribution to the match outcome is governed by the approved formula. Preserve it for audit and use it for an additional award or seeding purpose only when expressly authorized. | A standalone substitute for the full published match-outcome calculation, unless that formula expressly specifies it. | Role clarified in the v3.21 successor; exact workbook behavior remains unverified. |
| **Bracket advancement** | Progression in the recurring tournament bracket based on the verified match outcome, calculated from the published individual-performance metrics and approved scoring method, with D21 tie-breaks applied where needed. | A single raw Damage rank, `dblns` output, or overview average used without the approved formula. | Governing rule in the v3.21 editorial candidate; implementation tests remain blocked. |
| **Individual award / award eligibility** | Award outcome defined by the event notice and applicable rules, including recipient, criteria and values. | Team win/loss or a dollar estimate without an approved basis. | Preserve explicit event-specific requirements; no values invented. |
| **Event Settings Record** | Event-specific record identifying approved choices, version, effective time, responsible roles, and required settings. | A reusable template or unsigned approval form. | Clarified across companions. |
| **Event Settings Sheet (T01)** | Operations Manual template of configurable event settings. | Completed event-specific record or approval. | Use with qualification when ambiguity is possible. |
| **Event Settings and Publication Approval Record** | Companion working form for event-specific settings, file identity, approvals and release disposition. | Proof of approval merely because a record exists. | Draft, not approved; missing fields remain blockers. |
| **Selected working policy** | A selected policy direction documented for future approved implementation; it is not proof of organizer ratification or a working feature. | Implemented/verified policy. | Used in technical and companion wording. |
| **Historical validation result** | Evidence from a prior file, build or controlled test. | A passing result for v3.19, the canonical workbook, or an unavailable runtime. | Historical scope kept explicit. |
| **Verified implementation** | Behavior actually tested against the identified version/build in the required target environment with evidence. | A proposed procedure, formula-engine-only test, or pass from another artifact. | No unverified implementation claims added. |
| **BLOCKED / NOT TESTED** | A check could not be performed because a required source, tool, environment or authorization was unavailable. | PASS or successful certification. | Kept for workbook, HTML and runtime checks. |
| **WAIVED / OUT OF SCOPE** | A check intentionally excluded under an explicit scope decision. | A test that passed. | Must remain distinct from test results. |
| **NO-GO / NOT APPROVED** | Required gate evidence or authorization remains open; not suitable for event issue, scoring certification, or live deployment. | A final release approval. | Preserved in the release gate record. |
| **H12 diagnostic** | Historical display result based on `0.54 × dblns / 100`; not a price, cash amount, prize value, store valuation or guaranteed payout. | Official award or verified monetary conversion. | Clarified and kept non-public by default. |
| **Round 3 time metric** | Selected working policy stores positive elapsed seconds with per-game metric `1 / elapsed_seconds`, so lower time produces a higher metric; implementation remains open. | Round 4 historical time term. | Separate definitions preserved. |
| **Round 4 time term** | Historical scoring term used in the archived formula shape; it must not be inferred from Round 3 policy. | Round 3 selected time metric. | No unsupported equivalence introduced. |
| **Pilot Event / Recurring format** | Separate event formats with their own published rules and exceptions. | Applying a Pilot rule automatically to recurring season or vice versa. | Reaffirmed in companion rulesets. |
| **Cross-publication reference** | A reference to another publication explicitly names that publication and the target section/appendix where needed. | An ambiguous “Chapter 3” or “Appendix P” without its target publication. | Standardized in identified cross-publication passages. |
