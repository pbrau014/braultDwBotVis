## Governing match-outcome rule

**Match outcome is calculated from individual performance across applicable published game metrics (including Damage and Kills) under the approved scoring method; the verified outcome determines bracket advancement, with published tie-breaks applied.** The outcome calculation, accepted participant metrics, participant-level calculation outputs (including `dblns`), verified match outcome, bracket decision, and any separately authorized awards must remain distinct but traceable. Raw Damage/Kills alone, a participant-level output by itself, a manually asserted result, or an unverified calculator display must not substitute for the approved match-outcome calculation. Published forfeit/void rules may define an outcome where ordinary performance scoring does not apply.

This is a governing documentation rule for every relevant command, response, message, announcement, interface action, and staff operation. Operations and bot-maintainer guidance require bracket progression to rely only on a verified outcome calculated under the approved scoring method and published aggregation, rounding, and tie-break rules. When evidence or outputs conflict or cannot be verified, pause the affected publication and bracket action, preserve evidence, and obtain an authorized ruling.

The authoritative workbook and Score Reporter HTML were unavailable. Therefore, the canonical formula, weighting, aggregation, rounding, and implementation parity remain unverified; no workbook/HTML, Excel/VBA, Office 2010, Windows, runtime, or live Discord tests are claimed as passed.
