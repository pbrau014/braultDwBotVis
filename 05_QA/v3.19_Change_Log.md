1. **Historical v3.19 bracket wording; superseded in v3.21:** v3.19 made the verified official match result and tie-breaks the advancement authority while describing `dblns` as separate. The v3.21 successor replaces that policy with a performance-based match-outcome calculation: accepted individual metrics (including Damage and Kills) feed the approved scoring method; the verified outcome governs advancement with published tie-breaks. This superseding rule is now aligned across active publications and companions; implementation remains unverified.
2. **Damage verification reference:** corrected Operations Manual §2.8 to point to Appendix P of the Participant Guide. Removed a duplicate “of the Participant Guide” phrase discovered in the first visual proof.
3. **Cross-publication references:** named the destination publication for the identified chapter/appendix references in the Participant Guide, Operations Manual, Technical Reference and companion materials. Kept source numbering intact.
4. **M06 claim:** normalized grammar to “You have to play in a division to increase your win rate.” Added a note that the source does not identify a verbatim citation and that the statement is a testable hypothesis, not an established result.
5. **Release Gate record:** renamed the document to “Release Gate Status and Reconciliation Record”; preserved historical v3.15 context and the present NO-GO disposition.
6. **Historical test/version text:** corrected the Technical Reference to say retained records must not be interpreted as new v3.19 test results. Updated the event approval form to identify the candidate v3.19 role-based publication set while preserving the v3.15 policy baseline and v1.1 draft companions.
7. **Pilot/Recurring authority wording:** made the source hierarchy explicit in these drafts; no event-specific dates, prices, organizer identities or approvals were filled in.
